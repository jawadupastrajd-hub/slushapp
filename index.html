FAQ

Good questions.

Is Slush free to use?

Yes. The wallet is free. Network fees and some protocol fees still apply when you transact.

Who holds my funds?

You do. Your keys stay on your device and we cannot access or move your assets.

What if I lose my phone?

Restore your wallet on any device with your recovery phrase. Keep it offline and never share it.

Are the rates guaranteed?

No. Rates change and every strategy carries risk, including loss of funds. This is not financial advice.
